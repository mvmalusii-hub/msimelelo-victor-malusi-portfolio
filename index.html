Msimelelo Victor Malusi is a dynamic administrator, web developer, and business compliance & branding strategist. He is the Founder & CEO of Legacy Launchpad — a consultancy helping entrepreneurs build sustainable brands — and the Admin Manager at Liwelile Impumelelo Community Centre, driving operational excellence and community impact.

With a hybrid background in tech and organisational management, Victor bridges the gap between digital solutions and business integrity. He has helped over 50+ small businesses formalise their compliance, build professional websites, and develop cohesive brand identities.

Victor is passionate about empowering local enterprises and community organisations through accessible technology and sound business frameworks. He holds certifications in project management, web development, and corporate governance.
