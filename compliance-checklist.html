Business Compliance Checklist

A step‑by‑step guide for South African SMEs (Updated 2026).

✅ 1. Entity Registration

Register with CIPC – Name reservation, registration number, MOI (if required).

💰 2. Tax & Financial Compliance

SARS registration – Income tax (IT77), VAT (if turnover > R1M), PAYE, UIF, SDL. Also open a business bank account.

📜 3. Industry Licences & Permits

Check sector requirements – Health, transport, food. Apply for municipal trading licence.

🏆 4. BEE & Employment Equity

BEE certificate – Exempted Micro Enterprise (EME) or QSE certificate from accredited agency. For >50 employees, register with Employment Equity Commission.

📁 5. Record Keeping & Governance

Maintain records – Financials (5+ years), statutory registers, annual returns with CIPC, annual financial statements (if required).

🔄 6. Ongoing Obligations

Tax submissions – Provisional tax (2x/year), annual income tax (ITR14), VAT returns (monthly/bimonthly), monthly UIF & SDL payments.

Disclaimer: This is a general guide. Always consult a registered compliance officer for your specific needs.
